The guide

Your M-Pesa paybill or till, on one screen.

Daraja Studio shows the payments that arrive on the number you already own, sends money out from it, keeps the people and businesses you pay, and repeats Safaricom’s own words when something fails. You run it yourself, with your own Daraja app.

What it does

  • Money in. Every payment on your number, with the account number it quoted and the payer’s name where Safaricom sends one. QR codes, a prompt on the payer’s phone, invoices they can pay, and standing orders.
  • Money out. Send to a phone, one at a time or in a list. A second person releases a send above the amount you set, and Studio can ask Safaricom to send a payment back.
  • The record. History, statements and balances in Safaricom’s words, the week’s numbers, and the check that finds a payment Safaricom has and Studio does not.
  • Who can do what. People and roles, and your password again before anything that moves money or changes who can log in.
  • Other systems. Keys, webhook addresses and a record of every delivery, plus an inbox another system can post its own confirmations to.
  • Setup. One question per screen: your shortcode, your Daraja app, your operator, your passkey. Sandbox first, with pretend money, until you are ready for the real one.

Start with your own paybill

Four things are needed: your own paybill or till number, a Daraja app from Safaricom’s developer portal, a server that can run Docker, and an https address Safaricom can reach. Sending money out, and prompting a payer’s phone, each need one more thing from Safaricom, and the setup asks for those only if you turn them on.

The code is at github.com/kepas-tech/daraja-studio, free to use, change and share under the Apache Licence 2.0. Bring it up with Docker Compose and open the address; the first-run setup asks one question per screen, and the guide shows where each answer comes from on Safaricom’s two portals, and what still has to be done there.

Your shortcode stays yours, and the money settles in your own M-Pesa account.
